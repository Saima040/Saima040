## 💌 My Research Notebook

<div align="center">

### 🌸 Current curiosity

**Can we make AI models more robust without sacrificing too much clean-data accuracy?**

</div>

My learning roadmap:

- 🌱 **Foundations:** Strengthen my understanding of ML and deep learning.
- 🔍 **Investigation:** Learn how to evaluate models and identify weaknesses.
- ⚔️ **Experiments:** Explore adversarial attacks in controlled environments.
- 🛡️ **Defense:** Study techniques that may improve model robustness.
- 📚 **Documentation:** Record experiments, results, limitations, and lessons learned.

<details>
<summary>🌸 My research principles</summary>

<br/>

- Reproducible experiments matter.
- Results should be reported honestly.
- A failed experiment can still teach something useful.
- Robustness requires careful evaluation, not assumptions.
- Security research should be conducted responsibly.

</details>
